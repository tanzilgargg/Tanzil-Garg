# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (26 November 2026)

First public release of the rebuilt game.

### Added
- Restaurant hub that builds up room by room as lessons are completed.
- Seven worlds with 42 lessons, each world with its own guide character.
- Typing engine with live feedback, WPM, CPM, accuracy, combos and an on-screen finger guide.
- Three gameplay modes: sequence, multi-ticket service and typed decisions.
- Typing races against AI opponents and a ghost of your personal best.
- Planet Health system with an environment that changes around the restaurant.
- Daily eco missions with an Eco Chest, weekly missions, 30 achievements and a weekly league.
- Shop with restaurant themes, wallpaper, furniture, lighting, kitchen equipment and outfits for the player and for Cy.
- Restaurant Licence: a six-stage final shift with a printable certificate.
- Settings for sound, motion, text size, contrast, difficulty and hearts; backup and restore of progress.
- Offline support and installable web app.

### Checked
- Food safety and recycling content against FSA, WRAP / Recycle Now and GOV.UK guidance.
- Accessibility with axe-core on every screen.
- Difficulty at 10, 15 and 20 words per minute.
